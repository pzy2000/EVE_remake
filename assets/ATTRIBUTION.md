## Re-fetching

Run `tools/fetch_assets.sh` to re-download the MillionthVector images.
The Kenney Particle Pack zip is linked from https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack
(extract the "PNG (Transparent)" folder into `assets/fx/`).
